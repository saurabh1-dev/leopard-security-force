Our History

OUR JOURNEY

From a local security supplier to a nation-wide elite security force. Here is how we grew over the years.

2019

Company Founded

Leopard Security Force was established with a team of 50 trained guard agents in New Delhi, focusing on local retail and residential protection.

2020

Regional Expansion

We extended our security coverage into more metro and industrial zones, strengthening our guard deployment and client support network.

2021

Technological Shift

Integrated state-of-the-art AI-powered CCTV surveillance systems, UAV/drone patrolling, and built our Central Monitoring control room.

2024

Trusted Nationwide Presence

Expanded across premium commercial and residential projects with a stronger training matrix and faster client response systems.

2026

India's Trusted Force

Now trusted by over 500+ premium corporate partners and housing 2,000+ active, elite-trained agents nationwide.
