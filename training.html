Academy Syllabus

CORE TRAINING SYLLABUS

Our training program goes beyond standard parameters to cultivate situational awareness, defensive capability, and administrative professionalism.

Physical Fitness & Combat

Every personnel undergoes morning drill routines, obstacle courses, aerobic endurance tests, and specialized unarmed self-defense command checks to guarantee maximum operational stability.

Fire Protection & Suppression

Comprehensive drills on fire classification, handling distinct extinguishers types, electrical spark emergency audits, crowd panic release, and coordinated local fire squad support.

Access Audits & Logbooks

Personnel learn visitor app management, badge verification checkpoints, vehicle search mechanisms, delivery tracking registers, and maintaining exact log reports for safety managers.

Emergency First Aid / CPR

Certified life-saving instruction covering Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation (CPR), trauma pressure banding, stroke response, heatstroke first aid, and swift stretcher evacuation.

Soft Skills & Conflict Management

Developing verbal de-escalation skills, visitor reception hospitality, emergency squad radio commands, and public relations protocols for IT campuses and high-end residential societies.

Incident Writing & SOP Audits

Detailed coaching on daily incident report writing, logging perimeter breaches, vehicle parking offense tags, inventory shift tracking, and night supervisor communication logs.
